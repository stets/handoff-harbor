A finished WordPress site can still become a support mess when the client does not know who owns Beaver Builder licenses, where saved rows and templates live, how the theme controls global styles, or which edits are safe.

A WordPress login is not a complete Beaver Builder handoff.

The messy version: the agency owns the Beaver Builder license, premium add-ons depend on somebody else's account, saved modules are undocumented, form notifications still use a test inbox, and the client assumes every post-launch edit is included.

This is not legal, tax, hosting, security, accessibility, or WordPress policy advice. It is a practical closeout checklist for documenting ownership, recurring costs, editing rules, support limits, and maintenance options before final acceptance.

What to document before acceptance

Ownership and licenses

Record WordPress, hosting, registrar, DNS, recovery email, 2FA, Beaver Builder plugin/theme/add-on license owners, renewal dates, and whether freelancer access remains.

Builder structure

Map the active theme, Beaver Themer layouts, saved rows/modules/templates, global settings, breakpoints, reusable content, custom CSS/JS, and any shortcode or custom-field dependencies.

Forms and operations

Test forms, recipients, SMTP, spam controls, analytics, pixels, backups, caching, CDN, security, staging, plugin updates, and the rollback process.

Editing and support

Show safe text/image/page edits; flag templates, Themer layouts, global settings, plugins, custom code, caching, and responsive changes as higher risk; date the support boundary.

Copy/paste Beaver Builder handoff checklist

  • ☐ WordPress admin owner, user roles, recovery email, 2FA, hosting, registrar, DNS, SSL, email provider, and renewal contacts are documented.
  • ☐ Beaver Builder plugin, theme, Themer, and add-on license owners, account locations, renewal responsibilities, and transfer limits are recorded.
  • ☐ Active theme, child theme, global styles, breakpoints, menus, headers, footers, custom CSS/JS, and sitewide defaults are summarized.
  • ☐ Saved rows, columns, modules, templates, Themer layouts, reusable content, custom fields, shortcodes, and dynamic connections are explained.
  • ☐ Forms, SMTP, recipients, autoresponders, spam protection, CRM/newsletter links, analytics, pixels, Search Console, and conversion events are tested.
  • ☐ Caching, CDN, image optimization, backups, security, staging, updates, monitoring, and rollback each have a named owner.
  • ☐ Mobile, tablet, desktop, browser, link, redirect, 404, form, basic accessibility, and accepted known-issue checks are reviewed.
  • ☐ Client training separates safe content edits from risky global, template, Themer, plugin, custom-code, cache, and responsive edits.
  • ☐ Included support has an end date; new pages, redesigns, SEO/copy, integrations, plugin work, troubleshooting, and maintenance route to paid work.
